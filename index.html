Collection and search infrastructure for discovering second-hand and archival fashion. Vendor listings are normalised, images processed and garments connected through text search, image similarity and recommendations.

Technical details
Collection at scale

Collection systems convert different vendor formats into a consistent catalogue. The daily figures describe processing volume and new listings, rather than active inventory.

Image similarity

Image embeddings represent the appearance of garments. Vector search compares those representations across vendors to retrieve visually similar pieces for image search and product recommendations.
